# Pixora – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

Pixora – Screen Capture Studio ("Pixora") is a browser extension for taking screenshots, recording
the screen, and annotating the results. This policy explains what Pixora handles and where it goes.

## Summary

**Everything stays on your computer.** Pixora has no account, no server, no analytics and no
advertising. It never uploads your screenshots, recordings or any other data, and it does not sell
or share data with anyone.

## What Pixora handles, and where it is kept

| Data | When | Where it stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screenshots and screen / tab / camera recordings | Only when you start a capture or recording | Pixora's storage in your browser (IndexedDB), and files you choose to save to your computer |
| Microphone and camera audio / video | Only while you record with them turned on | Inside the recording, on your computer |
| Annotations, captions, chapters, privacy regions | When you edit a capture | With the capture, on your computer |
| Flow Capture steps (which control you clicked, page titles, and — only if you allow it — non-sensitive form values) | Only while a Flow Capture runs | With the flow, on your computer. Passwords and fields that look sensitive are always masked |
| Where you click while recording with click highlights on (left / right / double click and the mouse position) | Only while you record with *Highlight mouse clicks* on | Drawn on the page for a moment so the recording shows it; never stored or sent |
| Text found by OCR / sensitive-text detection | Only when you ask for it | Processed on your computer by the built-in OCR engine; nothing is sent anywhere |
| A picture of the browser window's top bar — tabs, address bar and bookmarks bar (optional) | Only when you turn on *Include browser header* and choose the window in Chrome's share dialog | Added to that screenshot, on your computer; the window picture is used once and not kept |
| Settings and preferences, including your theme | When you change them | Chrome's extension storage on your computer |
| The folder you choose for saving (optional) | When you pick one in Settings | A handle to that folder, in your browser; you can remove it in Settings |
| A picture you add from a web address (optional) | Only when you type or paste the address in *Add image › From a web address* | Pixora downloads that picture from the address (without your cookies) and keeps it with your captures. Like any download, the site you named receives the request; Pixora sends it nothing else |

In the Chrome Web Store's terms, Pixora therefore handles three kinds of user data — **website
content** (what you capture), **user activity** (the clicks and steps of a Flow Capture, and the
mouse position and clicks used for click effects and zoom while you record) and **web history** (the
address and title of each page you capture, with the time) — only on your computer, only for
capturing, and never sends them anywhere. Pixora does **not** ask for or keep personally identifiable
information, health, financial, authentication or location data, or personal communications, and it
does not track what you browse outside the captures you make.

## Sharing

Pixora never sends data to the developer or to third parties. A capture leaves your computer only
when **you** do it yourself — for example by saving a file, copying to the clipboard, or creating a
PDF, Word document or QR code and sharing that.

## Website access

Pixora reads a web page only when you invoke it on that page (toolbar button or keyboard shortcut),
to capture it. Access to other sites is never required. Chrome asks you for it — and you can
decline — only when you start something that needs it: a Flow Capture that follows you across pages
and sites, or adding a picture from a web address (access to that one site, to read the picture).

## Keeping and deleting data

Captures stay until you delete them, or until the automatic clean-up you choose in
Settings › Storage removes them. Removing the extension deletes all of Pixora's stored data.

## Children

Pixora is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the extension update and the date
above will change.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support contact on Pixora's Chrome Web Store page.
